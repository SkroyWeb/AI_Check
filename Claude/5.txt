The Student Who Kept Going

The clock says two, the page is blank,
The coffee's cold, the coursework's high.
He feels his confidence grow thin
As doubt keeps whispering, *"Why try?"*

He sees his friends all running ahead,
Their scores and smiles on every screen,
And wonders if he's just too slow
For the dreams he's carried since eighteen.

He studies hard and still falls short,
A red mark staring from the sheet.
He swallows hard, he looks away,
And hides the sting of that defeat.

There's pressure from the ones who love him,
Who hope he'll be what they have planned,
And weight he never learned to name
That sits like stone inside his hand.

Yet when the morning light comes through
And that same tired desk appears,
He opens up his books again,
Still wiping off last night's tears.

For something small refuses to die,
A stubborn spark behind his eyes.
It says, *"You're not behind, just growing,
And every fall is how you rise."*

So here's to all who've doubted, stumbled,
Who learned in silence, tried once more.
The shine you carry through the struggle
Is worth far more than any score.

After Edit
I kept it to struggles most students know: late nights, comparison, failure, family expectations, and self-doubt. I can make it shorter, more personal in first person, or more hopeful in tone if you'd like.
